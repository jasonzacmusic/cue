Google would not sign you in here

Google only accepts its sign-in form in a short list of browsers it knows by name. Cue is not on that list, so it answers “this browser may not be secure” however Cue describes itself. Your password is fine, and nothing on this Mac is wrong.

You are already signed in to Google in Chrome on this Mac. Cue can carry that sign-in across, for Google’s own sites and nothing else, into this identity only. Chrome is left exactly as it is, and your Mac may ask you once for permission to read it.
