Google would not sign you in here

Not your password. Google decides whether it trusts the browser before it lets you type anything, and for a long while it did not trust Cue: it looks at window.chrome, which a real Chrome fills with half a dozen things and Electron leaves empty. Cue now carries the same ones, and a personal gmail.com address that was refused every time goes straight through.

So reaching this page again is unusual. The likeliest cause left is old sign-in remains in this folder — Google ties a sign-in to the one browser it was made in, and anything carried over from Chrome was cancelled soon after. The button empties this folder’s Google cookies and begins again.

You will be asked for the password for this folder’s own account. If Google then offers a passkey or asks to verify the account, that is the account asking, not a refusal.
