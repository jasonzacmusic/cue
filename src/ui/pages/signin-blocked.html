Google would not sign you in here

This is almost always old cookies, not Cue and not your password. Google keeps a sign-in tied to the one browser it was made in, so the copy Cue used to take from Chrome was cancelled soon after — and the cancelled remains sit in this folder and make Google refuse before you can type anything.

Cue has since been tested against Google’s own sign-in from a clean start: Google serves the normal form and reaches the password step quite happily. So the cure is to empty this folder’s Google cookies and begin again, which is exactly what the button does.

You will be asked for the password for this folder’s own account. If Google then asks to verify the account, that is the account asking, not a refusal, and the code arrives on your phone.
