Google did not finish signing you in

Nothing you typed was wrong. Google stopped part-way, and Cue has already cleared this folder’s old Google sign-in once and tried again for you. These usually sort it out:

  1. Check the date and time on this computer. Google’s sign-in fails when the clock is off by more than a few minutes.
  2. Wait a minute, then try again. After several tries in a row Google sometimes asks for a pause.
  3. Use a different Google account if this one keeps stopping here, or if this folder should hold another account.

Only this folder is touched. Your other folders, and their Google accounts, stay signed in.
