Google would not sign you in here

Not your password, and not this folder. Google allows a Workspace address — one on your own domain — to sign in from a browser like Cue, and turns a personal @gmail.com address away before you can type anything. Tested side by side in the same empty folder, seconds apart: the school address reached the password step, the gmail.com one landed here.

That is Google’s rule about which browsers it trusts with a consumer account, not a fault in Cue, so it happens on any computer and clearing cookies does not change it. The button below still empties this folder’s Google cookies and starts again, which is worth one try if a Workspace address was refused.

For a personal gmail.com address there are two honest ways round it: read it inside the Workspace account you can sign into, by adding it under Gmail’s own Accounts settings — or keep that one inbox in Chrome.
